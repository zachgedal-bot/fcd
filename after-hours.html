Props Desk shot lines → expected shots → xG → goals

One player per line: name, pos, line, over odds, under odds[, anytime scorer odds] or name, pos, shots=1.8. Each line becomes the Poisson mean that reproduces the de-vigged over price, times an xG-per-shot by position (FW .12, AM .10, MF .08, WB .06, DF .05), summed and divided by the share of team xG the listed players carry. The props-implied goal expectancies are blended with the market's and re-priced against the main line, totals and scorer odds.

House Rules read before you sit down

Every number on this floor is a model output, not a measurement. The physiology anchors are peer-reviewed; the conversion from running lost to goals, the residual share, the goals-per-rating-point and the xG-per-shot defaults are assumptions until the backtest in altitude_edge/backtest.py says otherwise.

The market is the baseline. Every desk starts from the de-vigged closing line and only moves it by the piece of information you are claiming the market lacks. If your residual share is 1.0 you are claiming the book prices Toluca like Tijuana. It does not.

Odds are what you typed. Nothing here is live. Pull lines with the fetchers in altitude_edge/ on your own machine, or drop a CSV.

EV before limits. Bolivian, Ecuadorian and Peruvian league limits at sharp books are small. An edge you cannot get down is a hobby.

The rope is velvet, not a lock. The invite code is a front-end nicety. Do not put anything behind it that needs protecting.

Courtsiding has a house too. Venue terms, exchange rules and state law all apply. The calculator prices the trade; it does not bless it.
