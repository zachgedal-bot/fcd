Acclimatisation curve used by the model

Fraction of the acute deficit recovered vs days since arrival (fast ventilatory/plasma-volume phase + slow haemoglobin-mass phase). About 35% of the acute deficit is never recovered by a sea-level native at that altitude. Curve shape is an assumption calibrated to E19, E02, E03 and E06.

Ball flight (physics, not physiology)

Drag and Magnus (curve) forces are proportional to air density (E29, E16). Both teams face the same ball, but the team that trains at the match altitude is used to it. Point-mass model with quadratic drag, Cd = 0.25, 25 m/s launch at 20°; illustration only.

How the numbers are made

PHYSIOLOGY LAYER (relative to the same team at its home altitude)
vo2(alt) = −6.3% × max(0, alt − 300 m) / 1000 m [E18, measured]
acute = vo2(match) − vo2(home) if match > home, else 0 [no penalty going down; no validated bonus]
recovered = 0.40·(1−e^(−d/3.5)) + 0.25·(1−e^(−d/21)) [ASSUMPTION calibrated to E19, E02, E06]
VO2max Δ = acute × (1 − recovered)
HIR Δ = hirF × VO2max Δ (hirF default 0.7) [anchored to E03, E04, E31]
RSA Δ = rsaF × VO2max Δ (rsaF default 0.3) [bounded by E22–E24]
Sprint Δ = sprK × (match − home)/1000 m (sprK default +0.3%) [E26, E27; E04 found no top-speed change]
Technical, decision-making = insufficient evidence → no number [E13, E04, E28]

RATING LAYER (display convention only)
category Δ = clamp( sensitivity × Σ weight_i × physiology_i , ±15 )
PAC = 0.5·Sprint + 0.5·RSA · DEF = 0.5·HIR + 0.5·RSA · PHY = 0.3·VO2 + 0.7·HIR
STAMINA = 0.4·VO2 + 0.6·HIR · REPEATED EFFORT = RSA · SPRINT SPEED = Sprint
SHO, PAS, DRI = insufficient evidence (shown unchanged, greyed)
A 10% physiological change ≠ a 10% rating change; with sensitivity 0.4 it is −4 points.
